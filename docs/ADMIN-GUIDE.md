# Your dashboard — a one-page guide

Everything on your site is edited at **yoursite.com/admin**. Changes go live within seconds.
Nothing you do here can break the design: the layout, colours and type are fixed, and the
forms warn you before text gets too long.

---

## Signing in

Use your email and password. Accounts are invite-only — nobody can sign up.
Change your password under **Settings → Account**. If you're ever locked out, whoever looks
after the code can reset it for you.

---

## Adding a new project (about 2 minutes)

1. **Projects → New project**
2. **Title** — this shows on the card and the project page.
3. **Cover** — pick one:
   - **Upload** — drag a file in, browse, or paste an image with ⌘V
   - **Image URL** — paste a direct image address and press *Fetch*
   - **YouTube** — paste any YouTube or Shorts link
4. **Alt text** — one short sentence describing the artwork, for people using a screen reader
   and for Google. Tick *Decorative image* only if it carries no meaning.
5. **Categories** — pick at least one. These become the filter buttons on your work page.
6. **Publish**.

If something is missing, Publish tells you exactly what — it never fails silently.

> **Lots of work at once?** Scroll to **Bulk upload** at the bottom of the Projects page, drop
> in a folder of files, and choose *one draft project per file*. Everything arrives as a draft,
> so nothing goes public until you say so.

## The three ways to add any image or video

Every image slot on the site works the same way.

| | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Upload** | Files on your computer or phone. JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, SVG — up to 20 MB. Big files are resized for you; transparent PNGs stay transparent; GIFs keep animating. |
| **Image URL** | A direct image address. Google Drive links work if the file is shared *"Anyone with the link"*. Dropbox links work as-is. By default a copy is saved to your library, so your portfolio doesn't break if the original disappears. |
| **YouTube** | Any watch, youtu.be, Shorts or live link. Shorts become tall 9:16 cards, everything else 16:9. The video only loads when a visitor clicks, so your pages stay fast. |

**If a link doesn't work**, the message tells you why. The most common one: an Instagram or
Behance *post page* is a web page, not an image — open the image itself, right-click it,
choose *Copy image address*, and paste that instead.

## Arranging your portfolio

- **Order** — Projects → the ↑ ↓ buttons next to each project → *Save order*. That's the order
  visitors see.
- **Featured** — star up to three projects. They appear as large rows above the gallery.
- **Hide something** — select it and choose *Unpublish*. It stays in your dashboard.
- **Layout** — Display settings controls columns, spacing, and how many projects load at a time.
  Masonry keeps every artwork at its own shape, which suits mixed work; the uniform grid crops
  everything to one shape.

## Your profile

| Where | What you change |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | The greeting, your name, what you do, where you are, and your portrait |
| **About** | The "Let's Connect" heading and your bio. `**Double asterisks**` make text bold. |
| **Experience** | Your roles. Tick *current role* to show "Present" instead of an end year. |
| **Tools** | The app icons in the Tools pill — square PNG or SVG works best |
| **Contact** | Which ways to reach you are shown |
| **Sections** | Reorder the whole page, rename chapters, or hide one. The 01/ 02/ numbers update themselves. |

---

## Availability

**Settings → Availability** drives the pill in the menu bar and in your About chapter.

- **Open for work** — the orange pill
- **Limited availability** — same pill, your own wording
- **Not taking work** — a quiet grey pill, or hidden completely if you prefer

Change the status and the wording any time; it's one field and one button.

---

## A few habits worth keeping

- **Alt text on everything.** It's how people using screen readers experience your work, and
  it's what Google reads.
- **Watch the counters.** They turn orange past the recommended length — that's a nudge, not a
  block. The hard limit stops you before anything can break the layout.
- **Your CV** lives in Settings. Replace the PDF there and every *Download CV* button updates.
- **Press ⌘S** to save any form.
- **Leaving a page with unsaved changes** always asks first.
